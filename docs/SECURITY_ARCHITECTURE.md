# FaujiNiwas Security & Data Privacy Architecture
1. **Service Identity Masking**: Service numbers and Aadhaar numbers are salted and hashed on device before reaching Firebase.
2. **DPDP Act 2023 Compliance**: Zero retention of scanned military identity cards post officer verification.
3. **Restricted Location Precision**: Garrison residential quarters coordinates blurred by 500 meters to prevent operational mapping.
