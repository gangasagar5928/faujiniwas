Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 9, 2026

🛡️ Privacy-First Foundation

Fauji Niwas is designed from the ground up to respect the privacy of India's Armed Forces. Unlike commercial housing portals, we do not track browsing patterns, sell personal data to advertisers, or share telephone records with commercial brokers.

📊 Data We Collect & Processing Purposes

  • Phone Number (OTP Authentication): Used solely to authenticate your session, prevent fraud, and verify defence community affiliation.
  • Listings & Housing Data: Property descriptions, rent amounts, photos, and approximate area coordinates. Uploaded photos are stripped of EXIF metadata (camera model, GPS precision) client-side before storage to maintain military operational security (OPSEC).
  • Encrypted Military ID Verifications: Official service cards and proofs uploaded for verification are encrypted client-side using hybrid AES-256 and RSA-2048 keys. Only designated administrators can decrypt and review these files during verification.
  • Peer-to-Peer Encrypted Chats: Chat messages are end-to-end encrypted on device using the Web Crypto API. Plaintext messages are never stored on central application databases.
  • Local Vault Documents (ECHS): Health cards and personal records in the health vault remain encrypted inside your device's browser IndexedDB and are never uploaded to remote servers.

🇮🇳 Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 Compliance

Fauji Niwas acts as a Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. As a Data Principal, you are entitled to:

  • Right to Access & Summary: Request a summary of personal data being processed.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: Modify inaccurate profile data or request complete erasure of your account, listings, and verification records.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: Direct access to our designated Grievance Officer for privacy concerns or complaints.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate another individual to exercise data rights in the event of death or incapacity.

⏳ Data Retention & Deletion Policy

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill service purposes or comply with statutory requirements:

  • Active Accounts: Account profile and published listings are maintained while your account remains active.
  • Verification Blobs: Encrypted verification images are purged once verification checks are complete.
  • Right to Erasure Process: You may submit an erasure request directly through your profile settings or by emailing faujiniwashq@gmail.com. Upon receipt, all associated personal database records will be permanently purged within 7 business days.

☁️ Third-Party Data Processors

To provide hosting, authentication, and secure storage, Fauji Niwas engages the following verified cloud infrastructure providers:

  • Google Cloud Platform / Firebase (Alphabet Inc.): Cloud hosting, encrypted database (Firestore), Firebase Authentication, and storage bucket services located in data centers within the Republic of India (Asia-South1 / Mumbai region).
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim: Public reverse-geocoding for cantonment proximity mapping (non-identifiable area strings only).
